NoVA NEWS

Privacy Policy

Effective November 11, 2026

This policy covers the NoVA News website and the NoVA News publishing tool (the "Tool"), a private application that NoVA News uses to upload its own videos to its own accounts on TikTok, YouTube, Facebook and Instagram. The Tool is operated by NoVA News, Northern Virginia, USA, and is not available to the public.

Information the Tool accesses

When the NoVA News account owner connects an account, the Tool receives an access token from that platform (TikTok, Google/YouTube or Meta) and the basic account information the owner approves, such as the account name, account ID and avatar. The Tool may also read the account's own posting settings and performance statistics (for example view and like counts on our own videos) to decide which clips to make next.

How information is used

Information is used only to upload NoVA News videos and captions to NoVA News accounts, to confirm uploads succeeded, and to review how our own videos perform. We do not use it for advertising profiles, and we do not sell, rent or share it with anyone.

Storage and security

Access tokens and upload logs are stored only on a private computer controlled by NoVA News. They are not stored on any website or shared server. Tokens can be revoked at any time from the connected platform's settings.

Information about other people

The Tool does not collect personal information about viewers or other users. Videos we publish show public government meetings that were broadcast publicly by the government body.

Website

This website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking, and does not collect any information from visitors.

Data deletion

To have any data held by the Tool deleted, or to disconnect it, email novanewsva@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion". We will delete stored tokens and related records within 30 days and confirm by email. You can also revoke access directly in your TikTok, Google or Facebook account settings.

Children

The Tool and this website are not directed to children under 13 and do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

We will post any changes to this policy on this page with a new effective date.
